Privacy

Privacy at FeatherReader

FeatherReader is built so there is very little of your data for it to hold. Your reading lives in your own PDS; this server keeps only a cache and your login session.

No account, no tracking

There is no signup and no password database. You sign in with your own atproto identity over OAuth, so FeatherReader never sees or stores a password. There are no analytics, no advertising networks, no third-party trackers, and no telemetry — nothing here profiles you or follows you around the web.

Your reading data lives in your PDS

Your subscriptions, folders, stars, and read-state are written as open-standard community.lexicon.rss.* records in your own PDS — not on this server. That data is yours: it stays under your control, and OPML export is always available so you can leave with it at any time.

What this server does hold

To run the reader — and, during the current closed beta, to gate access — the server holds:

  • A shared cache of public feed content it has fetched — articles from the feeds people subscribe to, not personal data.
  • A per-user cache of your subscriptions and read/star state, so the list loads without re-reading your PDS on every request.
  • Your OAuth session — access and refresh tokens, encrypted at rest on the server's private volume and used only to read and write your own PDS records on your behalf.
  • While FeatherReader is in closed beta: the list of atproto identities (DIDs) that hold a beta seat, and the invite codes used to grant them.

Network and logs

The site is served through Cloudflare, a CDN and proxy that processes connection metadata such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service. Operational server logs may briefly record request metadata for debugging and abuse-prevention; they are not used to profile you. The feed poller makes outbound requests to the feed hosts you subscribe to, on a schedule, to fetch new items.

Public feeds only

Because your PDS is public, FeatherReader supports public feeds only. A private or paid feed carries its secret in the URL, and this server never stores or writes such a secret — please don't subscribe with one.

Deleting your data

Sign out to drop the server-side session. Your canonical data lives in your PDS, so removing a subscription, star, or folder in FeatherReader deletes the corresponding record from your PDS; the server-side caches are transient. To remove everything, delete the community.lexicon.rss.* records from your PDS, then sign out and stop using the service.

Changes

This page will be updated as FeatherReader evolves. Material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date below.

Contact

Questions or concerns: reach FeatherReader on Bluesky or open an issue on GitHub. Security reports should use private vulnerability reporting, not a public issue.
