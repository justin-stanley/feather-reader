Reading these

Waiting to be polled is the backlog. A healthy instance clears it every minute or so, and it should hover near zero. A number that keeps climbing means the poller cannot service the feeds it already has, and feeds will update less often than the target interval.

Least recent poll is the worst staleness anyone here is currently seeing. If it drifts far past the target interval, the backlog above is the reason. It reads never whenever any feed has yet to be fetched at all — that is a worse staleness than any number, so it wins outright rather than being averaged away.

Failing counts feeds whose last fetch errored. These retry on a widening delay rather than every interval, so they are not part of the backlog above — which means a feed that breaks makes the backlog look better, not worse. That is why the two numbers are listed separately. The second figure, when present, is the feeds that have failed often enough to be retrying hours apart: usually gone rather than flaky.

Fetching says whether new content is being pulled at all. It reads paused when the cache has hit the size limit that protects the disk; nothing new arrives until the retention sweep frees space. Off means the poller is not running here at all, and starting means it has not finished a round since this instance booted. Reading, searching and everything already cached keep working in every case — but every other number on this page will drift while this row says anything but running, and this row is the reason.

What is not here

No user counts, no per-feed detail. This page is public, and none of that is anyone else's business — it also says nothing about who reads what. The failure counts above are totals: they say how many feeds are broken, never which ones, and never whose. Everything here is about the machine, not the people using it.
