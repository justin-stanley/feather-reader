Reading these

Waiting to be polled is the backlog. A healthy instance clears it every minute or so, and it should hover near zero. A number that keeps climbing means the poller cannot service the feeds it already has, and feeds will update less often than the target interval.

Least recent poll is the worst staleness anyone here is currently seeing. If it drifts far past the target interval, the backlog above is the reason. It reads never whenever any feed has yet to be fetched at all — that is a worse staleness than any number, so it wins outright rather than being averaged away.

What is not here

No user counts, no error rates, no per-feed detail. This page is public, and none of that is anyone else's business — it also says nothing about who reads what. Everything above is about the machine, not the people using it.
